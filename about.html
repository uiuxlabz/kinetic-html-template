// Our story

Founded on the belief that idle brands fade.

Kinetic started in 2015 as a two-person motion studio in a shared Portland loft. Our first job was a 30-second launch film for a bike shop — and the owner told us it was the first time his brand had ever felt fast.

That was the idea we built the studio on: strategy that gives direction, design that gives edge, and motion that gives speed. Today we're 27 people across strategy, brand, product and motion — still working in forward gear.
